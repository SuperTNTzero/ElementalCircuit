## Adaptation by Elemental Circuits Project, 2026-10-31

Original recordings have been edited: stereo-to-mono conversion, silence and tail
trimming, pitch shifts, filtering, layering, short reflections, dynamics control,
fades, and OGG/Vorbis encoding at 44,100 Hz. These are adaptations, not unmodified
recordings. No original author endorses this mod. Original packs are not included
in the binary release.

Each final OGG's license and constituent source files, SHA-256 hashes, author,
source URL and modification notice are recorded in
`assets/elementalcircuits/audio/sources.json`. Mixes containing ViRiX material and
the project's contributions to those mixes are licensed under CC BY 3.0. CC0-only
mixes and the project's contributions to those mixes are offered under CC0 1.0.
Audio permissions apply independently of the project's source-code license.

Authoring recipes: `art/audio/recipes.json`. Source downloads and author-page
license evidence were recorded on 2026-10-31 in `art/audio/research/`.
